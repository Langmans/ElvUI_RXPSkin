# Changelog

## Unreleased

- ElvUI skin for RestedXP Guides (an ElvUI theme, ElvUI's borders on the guide window), Loreward
  and RestedXP Navigator, for WoW: Forever.
- First-run installer in ElvUI's plugin installer; options under `/ec` > RXP Skin.
- API for other plugins: `Configure`, `IsInstalled`, `ShowInstaller`.
- Eltruism's or WindTools' shadow round the skinned windows when that addon's shadows are on.
- RestedXP's step list scroll bar and its Active Targets frame skinned too.
- A translucent style: see-through windows at a chosen alpha, nested parts without backdrop.
- The guide window's shadow sits on its visible edge instead of 3 pixels outside it.
- Waymark's As Told panel on ElvUI's backdrop, with its own switch and installer page.
- The shadow switch works without a reload.
- A transparent style: no backdrop, border or shadow anywhere, only the text.
- Fonts: a SharedMedia font, outline and shadow for the text in the skinned windows; by default
  ElvUI's own general font and style, followed when they change.
- RestedXP's logo gives way to your class icon in an ElvUI frame, its cog in ElvUI's value colour.
- RestedXP's scroll bar shows only while the pointer is over the guide window.
- Loreward's progress bar like ElvUI's data bars: ElvUI's texture in its value colour.
- The see-through styles only for what stays on screen; windows you open keep ElvUI's skin.
- RestedXP's step list marks the current step and dims the finished ones.
- Mob, NPC and item names coloured in guide text that doesn't tag them.
